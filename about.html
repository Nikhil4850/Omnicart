Our Story

Founded in 2010, Westmire began with a simple mission: to create headphones that deliver exceptional sound quality while maintaining comfort and style. Our journey started in a small workshop in San Francisco, where our founder, Alex Westmire, combined his passion for audio engineering with innovative design.

Today, we're proud to be a global leader in premium audio technology, with our products being used by professionals and enthusiasts worldwide. Our commitment to quality and innovation continues to drive us forward.
